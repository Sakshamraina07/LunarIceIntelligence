2. WHAT WAS MEASURED, AND WHAT IT IS NOT
The quantity screened is derived from amplitude alone.
 With CPR built from the two amplitude channels, CPR and DOP are
monotone functions of the SAME single variable, so the rule 
CPR > 1.00 AND DOP < 0.13
 is arithmetically empty: satisfying the
DOP criterion bounds CPR from above, whatever the terrain. The screen is empty BY CONSTRUCTION, not by absence of ice.
It is therefore not a circular polarisation ratio.
 True CPR lives in the H

V phase, and taking magnitudes discards it. The
published quantity is reported here as a channel-imbalance proxy carrying CPR's name, which is what it is.
No machine-learning likelihood appears in this report.

random labels whose positive class was CPR 1.05
