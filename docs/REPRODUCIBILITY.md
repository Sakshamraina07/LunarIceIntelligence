## 5 · DN → σ⁰, exactly

`backend/scripts/process_real_sar_pipeline.py:405-411`, in this order:

```
sin_inc   = sin(deg2rad(inc))                      # per pixel, from the sri_in raster
K_lin     = 10 ** (K_dB / 10)                      # K_dB = 70.308868 (sri label)
sigma0_X  = (DN_X ** 2 * sin_inc) / (K_lin * G_X ** 2)
```

with G_LH = 1.018442, G_LV = 1.000923 (`gain_imbalance`, sri label). DN are
amplitude (METHODS §7.2, decided against the label's NESZ). sin θ and K are
common to both channels and cancel in every ratio; G_X does not
(`docs/propagation_percentiles.json` reports the residual of dropping sin θ:
|ΔCPR_a| max 1.31 × 10⁻², p50 2.3 × 10⁻⁵; zero pixels change screening state).
The SLC path (`backend/scripts/stokes_from_slc.py`) applies the same equation
with the **sli** label's K = 80.000000 dB and |E|² in place of DN².

## 6 · The screening quantities

After the 5 × 5 boxcar (§8) on σ⁰:

```
sqrt_lh, sqrt_lv = sqrt(boxcar(sigma0_lh)), sqrt(boxcar(sigma0_lv))
sigma_sc = 0.5 * (sqrt_lh - sqrt_lv) ** 2
sigma_oc = 0.5 * (sqrt_lh + sqrt_lv) ** 2
CPR_a    = sigma_sc / sigma_oc            (0 where S0 == 0)
DOP_a    = |S1| / S0, S0 = lh + lv, S1 = lh - lv, clipped to [0, 1]
```

(`process_real_sar_pipeline.py:425-431`.) Criterion: CPR > 1.00 AND
DOP < 0.13, both from Sinha et al. 2026 (METHODS §6.1). METHODS §1 proves
CPR_a = tanh²(x/4), DOP_a = |tanh(x/2)| with x = ln(LH/LV), so
CPR_a ≤ 0.004261082862785302 wherever DOP_a < 0.13.

## 9 · Filter kernel and boundary rules

Pipeline boxcar: `MULTILOOK_KERNEL = 5`, `cv2.filter2D(a, -1, ones(5,5)/25)`
with OpenCV's default border (`BORDER_REFLECT_101`)
(`process_real_sar_pipeline.py:109, 272-274`). The audit and bootstrap scripts
re-implement the same 5 × 5 mean with `scipy.ndimage.uniform_filter`
(`mode="nearest"` in `incidence_audit.py` / `propagation_percentiles.py`;
default `reflect` in `measure_enl.py` / `bootstrap_enl.py`) and with cumulative
sums under edge padding in `stokes_from_slc.py`; the boundary rules differ only
in the outermost two pixels of a window and every ENL is measured on a mask
eroded by that width (§7). CPR/DOP are formed on the boxcar output, never on
raw pixels.

## 10 · ENL patch parameters

From `docs/enl_estimator_spec.json` (read from the code by
`backend/scripts/enl_estimator_spec.py`): statistic mean²/var on DN²; patches
16 × 16 (headline), 32 × 32, 64 × 64; stride = patch, overlap 0, tiles anchored
at the raster origin, partial edge tiles dropped; a patch is used only if every
pixel is inside the mask; variance ddof = 1; estimate = mode of the per-patch
distribution located as exp(midpoint of the fullest of 120 equal-width bins of
ln(mean²/var)); mean and quantiles reported beside it. Benchmark of this
estimator at known ENL: `docs/enl_benchmark.json`.

## 11 · Bootstrap parameters

`backend/scripts/bootstrap_enl.py` → `docs/bootstrap_enl.json`: B = 2000,
seed 2026 (`numpy.random.default_rng`), estimator imported from
`measure_enl.py` unchanged; simple bootstrap resamples patches i.i.d.; block
bootstrap resamples whole rows of the 16 × 16 patch grid (one row = an azimuth
band 16 px = 400 m high spanning the range extent), pooling every finite
positive patch ratio in the drawn rows; interval = percentile 2.5 / 97.5, not
BCa. FP and floor are propagated through F(2N, 2N) at each replicate's ENL.

## 12 · Sub-band multilook control

`backend/scripts/slc_multilook_control.py --windows 9 --lines 8400 --patch 16
--guard 8` → `docs/slc_multilook_control.json`. Nine windows of 8400 azimuth
lines starting at `linspace(0, 355768 − 8401, 9)`; range bins cropped to those
non-zero down > 99.9 % of the window; azimuth FFT length 8400 after removing the
column mean; occupied band = bins with PSD > 0.05 of its maximum, centred on the
Doppler centroid found by a moving sum; 21 sub-bands of width
`len(band) // 21`; headline arm = hard rectangular split (no taper, no guard);
diagnostic arms: per-look power equalisation, Hamming taper across each
sub-band with 8 guard bins dropped at each edge, and both. Spatial arm =
intensity mean over 21 consecutive lines. Both decimated by 21. Resolution and
autocovariance of the two arms: `docs/mechanism_controls.json`
(`backend/scripts/mechanism_controls.py`).

## 14 · Sign convention for S₃ (documented parameter)

Stokes components from the coherency matrix: S₀ = l_H + l_V, S₁ = l_H − l_V,
S₂ = 2 Re⟨E_H E_V*⟩, and **S₃ = −2 Im⟨E_H E_V*⟩**; SC = (S₀ − S₃)/2,
OC = (S₀ + S₃)/2, CPR = SC/OC. This is the one binary choice the product admits
(the other sign gives CPR → 1/CPR); it is fixed by the definition of CPR —
single-bounce reflection reverses handedness and must give CPR < 1 — and by the
observation that the reading with median 0.2115 (not 4.7273) is the one under
which the south pole is single-bounce dominated. The inter-channel phase
clusters at −88.33° (resultant 0.9463 over 5 883 594 cells), 1.67° from
quadrature, bounding residual phase error to 0.043 % on |S₃|.
`docs/stokes_from_slc.json::sign_convention`; the same sign is used by
`joint_criterion.py`.
